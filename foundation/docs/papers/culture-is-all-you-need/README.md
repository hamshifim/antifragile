## Reading note: formal framework

The opening framework was read from the complete Drive text, not search snippets. Goals are sets of system states assigned by analyzing agents to acting agents. Strategy is the analyzing agent's evaluation of actions relative to the probability of transitioning from an origin state to attributed goals. Attribution may be mistaken; the acting agent need not possess explicit intentions, a plan, or a stable identity. Agent Steps Graphs describe states and actions; context constrains interpretation, and the Light Cone of Consequences bounds predictive scope. Doubt is the analyzer's subjective assessment of risk or model error. These are the author's definitions, not claims of empirical validation.

This research reference does not change the repository's operating contracts or assert that the proposed architecture is implemented.
